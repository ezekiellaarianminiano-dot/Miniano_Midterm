ABOUT

Art has always been a way for me to express the things that are difficult to put into words.

I am a Multimedia Arts student who is passionate about art, design, and visual storytelling. I enjoy exploring different creative mediums and turning ideas into something meaningful and visually expressive.

My interest in creativity began during my time in the Arts and Design Strand, where I discovered how much I enjoyed expressing myself through art and design. The strand helped me understand the importance of creativity, imagination, and having the courage to share my own ideas.

Now, as a Multimedia Arts student at Far Eastern University - Alabang, I continue to learn and grow as an artist. I hope to keep improving my skills, discovering my own style, and creating work that reflects who I am while connecting with others through creativity.
